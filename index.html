🌍 Community translation
Zimbabwe's 2013 Constitution (Section 6) recognizes 16 official languages: English, ChiShona, IsiNdebele, TjiKalanga, Chewa, Chibarwe, Khoisan, Nambya, Ndau, Shangani, Sotho, Tonga, Tswana, Venda, Xhosa and Zimbabwean Sign Language. This tracker offers all 15 of those that are text-based (Sign Language isn't — see the note below the language row). ChiShona, IsiNdebele, Setswana, Chewa, Shangani, Sotho, Venda and Xhosa ship with a best-effort draft translation of every UI string — none reviewed by a native speaker yet, treat it as a starting point. TjiKalanga and Tonga also ship a draft, but at lower confidence than the others — please prioritize reviewing those two. Chibarwe, Khoisan (Tjwao), Nambya and Ndau are intentionally still blank rather than guessed: this project doesn't have reliable enough source material to draft those four responsibly (Tjwao in particular has fewer than 10 living speakers and no published lexicon). Suggest corrections below for any of the 15; once a suggestion gets 2 community endorsements it's promoted to "verified" and goes live automatically. Oxford and Cambridge don't currently publish dictionaries for most of these languages, so please cross-check suggestions against ALRI (African Languages Research Institute, University of Zimbabwe) — the Duramazwi Guru reChiShona and Isichazamazwi SesiNdebele dictionaries — plus Hannan's Standard Shona Dictionary, South Africa's PanSALB resources for Sotho/Tswana/Venda/Xhosa, and open lexical resources like PanLex or Wiktionary where available. A native speaker's judgment always beats any dictionary here — this project doesn't claim these sources are authoritative for every language listed.
